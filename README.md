## PowerBI project of Iphone sales data
Power BI dashboard analyzing iPhone sales, revenue, profit, customer behavior, and model performance in India.
### Project Objective:
To use Business Intelligence and Data Visualization techniques to analyze iPhone sales data and generate actionable insights related to sales, revenue, profit, customers, and product performance.

This project focuses on analyzing iPhone sales in India using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides an interactive view of sales performance, revenue, profit, customer behavior, payment methods, and iPhone model performance.

The objective is to transform sales data into meaningful business insights that can support better decision-making.

### Tools & Technologies
Power BI – Dashboard and data visualization
Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
DAX – Calculated measures and KPIs
Data Modeling – Relationships and Star Schema
CSV / Excel – Dataset

### Key Insights
Identified the best-performing iPhone models based on sales and revenue.
Compared revenue and profit across different iPhone models.
Analyzed monthly and yearly sales trends.
Examined customer purchasing behavior.
Compared different payment methods such as UPI, Credit/Debit Card, and Finance Services.
Identified the models contributing most to overall sales performance.
### Business Recommendations
Focus marketing efforts on high-performing iPhone models.
Analyze customer preferences to improve product targeting.
Use sales trends to support inventory and demand planning.
Promote suitable payment and financing options to improve customer conversion.
Monitor model-level profitability rather than focusing only on sales volume.
